# Engineering Constitution -- Active Summary
Human owner retains release authority. Evidence outranks assertions. Private source work, independent review, provenance, and explicit decisions are the default.
- Verify source identity, behavior, compatibility and reviewer claims before making decisions.
- Technical readiness is NOT owner release authorization; no direct main pushes or unauthorized tags, merges or publication.
- Favor privacy, recovery, tester value, and review of the full defect class; document disagreements and exceptions.
